# One-Page Clip Permission Note (template)

> Not legal advice. A plain-language starting point for a streamer and an editor who work together. For paid or long-term work, have a lawyer review a full agreement.

**Date:** [date]
**Streamer (owner of the footage):** [name], channel [channel name and platform]
**Editor:** [name and contact]

1. **What this covers.** The editor may download and edit only these recordings: [list VOD links or file names]. Nothing else.
2. **Purpose.** The editor makes short vertical clips for the streamer. The editor does not post, sell, license or monetize the footage or the clips anywhere.
3. **The streamer confirms** they own the footage, or have the right to share it, including consent from anyone else who appears, and that they have checked the video policy of any game shown and cleared or removed any music that is not theirs.
4. **Do-not-clip list.** Segments the editor must skip: [timestamps or descriptions, or "none"].
5. **Who owns the clips.** The streamer owns the footage and the finished clips. The streamer decides where and whether to post them.
6. **Deletion.** The editor deletes the source files and working files within 30 days after the job ends, or sooner if the streamer asks in writing.
7. **Revoking.** The streamer can withdraw permission at any time in writing (email counts). Work stops right away.
8. **Tools.** If AI-assisted tools were used (for example for transcripts or captions), the editor says so on delivery.
