# Vertical Clip QC Checklist (10 points)

Run this before a clip goes out. Tick every box or fix it first.

1. [ ] The first second shows the moment or the hook text. No slow intro.
2. [ ] The clip starts and ends on a whole sentence or a natural pause.
3. [ ] The speech level is clear and not clipping. Game audio does not drown the voice.
4. [ ] Captions match what is said, are spelled correctly and stay inside the safe area (away from the very top and bottom of the frame).
5. [ ] The face and the action are both visible in the 9:16 frame.
6. [ ] Nobody appears without their OK (guests, co-streamers, people in the background).
7. [ ] No uncleared music, no private information, no visible usernames you do not want public.
8. [ ] The game's or sponsor's clip policy has been checked, and a sponsored segment carries the right disclosure.
9. [ ] The title and caption describe what really happens. No bait the clip does not deliver.
10. [ ] Any AI-generated or AI-altered element is labeled where the platform asks for it.

Log each clip in `rights-log.csv`.
